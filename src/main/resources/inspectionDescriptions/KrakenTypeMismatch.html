Reports two type incompatibilities that the engine rejects.

Comparison between incompatible types. Kraken compares numerics with numerics, dates with dates and date-times with date-times. Date and DateTime are not comparable with each other — the classic KEL trap, otherwise only visible when the engine runs. Money and Number are comparable, and Money is assignable to Number but not the reverse.

Incompatible function argument. The bundled catalogue carries the real KEL type of every built-in parameter, so a mistyped argument is reported the way the engine words it.

What it stays silent on. The plugin does not infer every expression type yet: operators, iteration variables and unresolved references all yield an unknown type, and any check touching one is skipped. Union types such as Date | DateTime and unresolved generics are treated as dynamic. Arguments of project-declared Functions are not checked, since they are commonly expressions the inference does not cover, and checking them would mostly produce noise.
